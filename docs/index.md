# STARTS Forward
### Shared purpose. Collective progress.

STARTS Forward connects students and early-career public
health professionals with communities, NGOs, universities,
and health systems to advance the Sustainable Development Goals.

## Meet STARTS Bridge
Our AI-powered assistant helps users explore evidence,
identify potential collaborators, and develop proposals
for paid, supervised public health projects.

## What we are building
- Evidence discovery using Exa
- Collaboration across countries and institutions
- Project planning connecting community needs to service delivery
- Career pathways for early-career professionals
- Human review and responsible AI governance

## Project status
The interactive application is currently a local prototype.
This website introduces the project and shares its code.
